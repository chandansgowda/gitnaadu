GitNaadu

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version: GitNaadu runs in your browser and needs no account. We only receive personal details if you choose to claim a certificate, and we use them to issue that certificate and to stay in touch about Engineering in Kannada.

Who we are

GitNaadu is a free Git learning playground by Engineering in Kannada. "We" and "us" in this policy mean Engineering in Kannada.

Using the playground

The terminal, the files you create, your repositories and your challenge progress are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us, and clearing your browser data or pressing Reset removes them.

What we collect for a certificate

When you finish every challenge and ask for your certificate, we ask you for:

  • your full name, as it should appear on the certificate
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • whether you are from a college or a company, and its name

Claiming a certificate is optional. If you would rather not share these details, you can keep using GitNaadu without one.

Why we collect it

  • to put your name on the certificate and keep a record of who completed the course
  • to understand who is learning with GitNaadu (students or working professionals, and from where)
  • to contact you about Engineering in Kannada courses, events and updates

We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with advertisers.

Where it is stored

The details you submit are sent to a Google Form and stored in Google Sheets, in a Google account managed by Engineering in Kannada. Google processes that data on our behalf under its own privacy policy. Only the Engineering in Kannada team can see the responses.

Your browser remembers only the name on your certificate, so it can be shown again later. Your email, phone number and college or company are not kept in your browser.

Your certificate link and QR code

Every certificate carries a QR code and a verification link. The link itself contains the name on the certificate and its date of issue, and opens a public page that shows the certificate. Anyone you give the certificate or the link to can see that name and date. Your email, phone number and college or company are never part of the link.

Other services

GitNaadu loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads, as with any website that uses them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep certificate details for as long as GitNaadu certificates are offered, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details you submitted, or to stop contacting you. Message Engineering in Kannada through our YouTube channel or LinkedIn and tell us the email address you used.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
